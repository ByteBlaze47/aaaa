The original particle *は* was retained while the noun *今日* contracted to *こ* + *んに*. The modern form is now a fixed greeting, not a shortened ellipsis.

neutral‑polite tone.  \n- **Formality:** Plain‑polite; acceptable in most everyday contexts, but not as formal as *こんにちは、○○様*.\n\n---\n\n### 5. 🗣️ Pronunciation tip  \n\n**Narrow IPA:** `[kóɴ·ni·tɕi·wa]`  \n\n- The first mora carries a high pitch (accent on *こ*).  \n- Keep the nasal *ん* lightly nasalised and let it flow into the following *に* (no glottal stop).  \n\n**Tip:** Say it as one smooth unit; avoid pausing between *ん* and *に* – it should sound like “konn‑i‑chi‑wa” with a gentle rise on the first syllable.\n\n---\n\n### 6. 🔄 Similar expressions  \n\n\p Expression \p Nuance / Usage \p\n\p------------\p----------------\p\n\p **こんばんは** – “Good evening.” \p Used from dusk onward; same structure, particle *は* after *晩*. \p\n\p **おはようございます** – “Good morning.” \p More formal; uses the polite verb *ございます* rather than a plain topic marker. \p\n\n---\n\n### 7. 💬 Potential responses  \n\n1. **こんにちは、元気ですか？** – “Good afternoon, how are you?” (friendly, conversational).  \n2. **こんにちは、はい、ありがとう。** – “Good afternoon, I’m fine, thank you.” (polite, brief reply).\n\n---\n\n### 8. ✂️ Shortcut / Full form  \n\n**Full historical form:** *今日は* (*kyō wa* → *konnichiwa*) – literally “as for today”.  \nThe modern greeting is now a lexicalised, fixed expression, so no further expansion is needed.
